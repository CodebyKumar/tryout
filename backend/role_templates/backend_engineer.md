Backend Engineer (2 to 4 years)
Summary: APIs, databases and production debugging for a product backend.

Backend Engineer, 2 to 4 years of experience, remote friendly.

You will build and run the APIs and services behind our product, working with a small team that owns what it ships.

What you will do: design and build REST APIs; model data in a relational database and write efficient queries; handle failures, retries and background jobs; write tests; investigate production issues from logs and metrics; review teammates' code.

Must have: 2 or more years building backend services in Python, Go, Java or Node. Solid SQL and schema design. Understanding of HTTP, authentication, and error handling. Has debugged a real production incident.

Good to have: message queues, caching, cloud deployment, observability tools, experience with payments or high-traffic systems.

Interviewer notes: prefer concrete stories over definitions. Ask what the candidate personally did, what the failure was and how they found it. Probe trade-offs they chose and what they would change.
