Sales Development Representative (0 to 2 years)
Summary: Outbound prospecting and qualifying leads for a B2B software team.

Sales Development Representative, 0 to 2 years of experience, hybrid.

You will find and start conversations with potential customers and pass qualified leads to account executives.

What you will do: research target accounts; write and send outbound emails and calls; handle objections; qualify leads on need, budget and timing; keep the CRM accurate; hit weekly activity and meeting targets.

Must have: strong communication, resilience after rejection, curiosity about customers' problems, basic comfort with spreadsheets and email tools.

Good to have: prior outbound or inside sales experience, familiarity with a CRM, interest in software or SaaS.

Interviewer notes: ask for a real example of persuading someone and what they did when told no. Role-play one short objection. Look for structure and honesty about results, not rehearsed answers.
