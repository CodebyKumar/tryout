Customer Support Agent (fresher or 1 year)
Summary: Written and spoken support for a software product.

Customer Support Agent, 0 to 1 year of experience, shift based.

You will help customers of a software product through chat, email and calls, and turn what you learn into better answers for the team.

What you will do: answer customer questions clearly and kindly; diagnose problems by asking the right questions; escalate bugs with a clear summary; keep notes and improve help articles; stay calm with upset customers.

Must have: clear spoken and written English, patience, a habit of checking facts before answering, comfort learning a new product quickly.

Good to have: previous support or retail experience, basic troubleshooting knowledge, experience with a ticketing tool.

Interviewer notes: use short scenarios (an angry customer, a bug you cannot fix right away). Judge empathy, clarity and how they ask questions, not product knowledge.
