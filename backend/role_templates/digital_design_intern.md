Digital Design Engineer Intern (fresher)
Summary: RTL design, FSMs and timing basics for a 6 month VLSI internship.

Digital Design Engineer Intern (fresher), VLSI design team, 6 months, hybrid.

You will work with senior engineers on RTL design and verification of small digital blocks for our SoC projects.

What you will do: write synthesizable Verilog or SystemVerilog for blocks such as FIFOs, counters, FSMs and simple bus interfaces; build basic testbenches and run simulations; debug waveforms; run lint and basic synthesis checks and fix what they report; document designs with block diagrams and timing assumptions.

Must have: B.E./B.Tech/M.Tech (or final year) in Electronics, Electrical or Computer Engineering. Strong digital fundamentals: combinational and sequential logic, flip-flops vs latches, FSM design. Working knowledge of Verilog or SystemVerilog, including blocking vs non-blocking assignments and synthesizable vs non-synthesizable code. Setup time, hold time and basic clock domain ideas. Hands-on with one simulator (ModelSim, Questa, VCS, Vivado or Icarus) through coursework or projects.

Good to have: a mini project such as a UART, SPI controller, ALU or FIFO; clock domain crossing and synchronizers; Python, Tcl or Bash scripting; Git; basic UVM or assertions.

Interviewer notes: this is a fresher role, so judge fundamentals and learning ability, not depth of experience. Ask them to explain a project they built, what broke and how they debugged it. Probe timing and FSM concepts with a small concrete example, not trivia.
