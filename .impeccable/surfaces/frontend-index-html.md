# Home page and sign in / sign up

Mode: Persuade. Audience: people reviewing the owner's work, from a resume or portfolio; they skim. Job: understand TryOut in one look and open the demo. Related: sign in and sign up share the world and must not feel like a bare form.

Constraints: honest copy only (PRODUCT.md); no portfolio-project line in the footer; few words; larger type than the previous version and less empty space.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page proves the mechanism instead of describing it: one real exchange from the demo turns into a verified score in front of the visitor. It refuses the split hero with a feature-card row.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white paper, deep ink, one cobalt signal for actions and the score bar, highlighter yellow only on quoted evidence. Familjen Grotesk display set very large and tight; IBM Plex Sans body at 18px. The transcript is a conversation ledger: speaker column, mono timestamps, ruled rows. The score is one bar.

STORY: The visitor reads a two-line claim, watches an answer get highlighted and scored, believes scores rest on what was said, and presses Try the demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 72px bar, mark plus wordmark left, Sign in and Try the demo right. Centred headline, two lines, about 76px; one deck line at 20px; Try the demo (primary) and Create account. Below, a 960px transcript panel: interviewer question, candidate answer; the highlighter sweeps one sentence, then the criterion row fills its bar to 7/10 with "Quote found in the transcript". Primary action: Try the demo in the hero.

FORM: Transcript to score; position 1 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:5e6e2dfd5fca8ff1. Signature interaction: the highlighter sweep that triggers the score fill, once, reduced-motion shows the end state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
